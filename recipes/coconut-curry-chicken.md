# Coconut Curry Chicken

Chicken thighs simmered in a coconut curry sauce with burst cherry tomatoes. A big pot that serves six and tastes even better the next day.

- **Method:** Induction stovetop, one pot
- **Time:** about 50 minutes
- **Serves:** 6
- **First made:** Not yet (planned November 2026)

## Ingredients

**Chicken**

- 3 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp avocado oil

**Sauce**

- 2 medium red onions, diced
- 1 1/2 tbsp minced garlic
- 1 1/2 tbsp minced ginger
- 3 to 4 tbsp Patak's curry paste (or the spice mix below)
- 1 1/2 pints cherry tomatoes (about 3 cups), halved
- 1 can (13.66 oz) unsweetened coconut milk
- 1 1/4 cups chicken broth
- 1 tbsp lime juice
- Pinch of garam masala to finish
- Salt to taste

**Spice mix (if not using curry paste)**

- 1 1/2 tbsp curry powder
- 1 1/2 tsp garam masala
- 3/4 tsp turmeric
- 3/4 tsp ground cumin

**To serve**

- About 6 cups cooked rice (2 cups dry)
- Sour cream

## Steps

1. **Prep.** Cut the chicken into 1-inch pieces and toss with the salt. Dice the onions and halve the tomatoes.
2. **Brown the chicken.** Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large pot over medium-high. Brown half the chicken, about 5 minutes, then move it to a plate. Repeat with the rest and the other 1 tbsp oil. It does not need to be cooked through yet.
3. **Soften the onions.** Lower the heat to medium. Cook the onions in the same pot until soft, 6 to 8 minutes, scraping up the browned bits.
4. **Bloom the curry.** Add the garlic, ginger, and curry paste or spice mix. Stir for 1 minute until it smells toasty.
5. **Burst the tomatoes.** Add the tomatoes and cook about 5 minutes, pressing them with the spoon so they burst.
6. **Simmer.** Pour in the coconut milk and the broth. Return the chicken and its juices. Simmer uncovered for 25 to 30 minutes, stirring now and then, until the sauce thickens.
7. **Finish.** Stir in the lime juice and a pinch of garam masala. Taste and add salt. Serve over rice with a spoon of sour cream.

## Notes

- Browning in two batches matters. A crowded pot steams the chicken instead of searing it.
- Sliced mini sweet peppers can go in for the last 5 minutes for more vegetables.
- Plain yogurt can replace the sour cream.
- Green onions or cilantro on top are a good finish if you have them.
- Keeps 4 days in the fridge and freezes well.
