# Bacon and Cheese Smothered Chicken Thighs with Griddled Broccoli and Peppers

Seasoned chicken thighs seared in bacon fat, topped with melted cheese and bacon, with charred broccoli and sweet peppers. Everything cooks on the griddle.

- **Method:** Blackstone griddle (oven variant below)
- **Time:** about 30 minutes
- **Serves:** 6
- **First made:** October 4, 2026

## Ingredients

**Chicken**

- 3 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- 3 slices bacon (up to 6 if you have them)
- 1 1/2 cups shredded Mexican cheese
- 1 tbsp cooking oil

**Seasoning rub**

- 1 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 3/4 tsp onion powder
- 1 1/2 tsp salt
- 3/4 tsp black pepper

**Veggies**

- 16 oz frozen broccoli florets
- 10 mini sweet peppers, halved and seeded
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 3 tbsp water (for steaming the broccoli)
- Pinch of salt

**Side salad**

- 6 cups chopped romaine lettuce
- 4 tbsp burger sauce (as dressing)

## Steps

1. **Prep and preheat.** Pat the chicken dry. Mix the rub and coat the chicken all over. Preheat the Blackstone with one side on medium-high and the other on medium, about 10 minutes.
2. **Cook the bacon.** Cook the bacon on the medium-high side until crisp, about 7 minutes, flipping once. Set it on a paper towel and spread the fat across that side of the griddle.
3. **Sear the chicken, first side.** Add 1 tbsp oil to the bacon fat. Lay the thighs on the medium-high side with space between them, press each one flat with a spatula, and leave them alone to brown, about 6 minutes.
4. **Start the veggies.** Put the other 1 tbsp oil on the medium side. Add the peppers cut side down and the broccoli straight from frozen, with a pinch of salt. Pour the water over the broccoli and cover with a dome or an upturned foil pan to steam, about 4 minutes.
5. **Flip the chicken, char the veggies.** Flip the thighs and cook until they reach 175°F inside, about 6 minutes. Uncover the veggies and toss them every minute or so until the broccoli has charred edges.
6. **Smother.** Turn the chicken side down to low. Chop the bacon fine, top each thigh with cheese and bacon, and cover with the dome until the cheese melts, about 90 seconds.
7. **Salad and serve.** Toss the romaine with the burger sauce. Rest the chicken a few minutes, then serve with the veggies and salad.

## Notes

- Go by the 175°F reading, not the clock. Thick thighs may need an extra 2 to 3 minutes per side.
- If the thighs will not all fit with room between them, cook in two rounds and hold the first batch on the cool edge of the griddle.
- No dome: a foil pan or a metal bowl works.
- Green beans or a California vegetable medley can replace the broccoli with the same timing.
- Kimchi is a good cold side. The tang cuts the cheese and bacon. Warm it on the griddle for a minute at the end if you like it hot.
- Artichoke hearts (drained and patted dry) can join the veggies for the last few minutes.
- Leftover thighs reheat well and are good chopped over romaine the next day.

## Oven variant

Heat the oven to 425°F. Crisp the bacon on a rimmed sheet pan (about 13 minutes), then toss the veggies in the bacon fat plus 1 tbsp oil on that pan. Put the chicken on a second pan, on the upper rack. Roast both about 22 minutes, stirring the veggies and swapping racks halfway, until the chicken reaches 175°F. Top with cheese and bacon and return to the oven for 3 minutes.

In a countertop convection oven, use 400°F and cook 1 to 1.5 lb of thighs per batch, 18 to 22 minutes each.

## Air fryer variant

Cook the thighs at 400°F for 16 to 18 minutes, flipping once, then add the cheese and bacon for the last 2 minutes. Work in batches so the basket is not crowded.
