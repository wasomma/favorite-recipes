# Keto Balsamic Vinaigrette

Tangy olive oil and balsamic dressing, cut with red wine vinegar to keep the carbs down. About 1 g net carbs per 2 tbsp serving.

- **Method:** No cook, shake in a jar
- **Time:** 5 minutes
- **Serves:** 6 (2 tbsp each, makes about 3/4 cup)
- **First made:** Not yet

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup extra virgin olive oil
- 2 tbsp balsamic vinegar
- 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1 small garlic clove, minced or grated
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano or Italian seasoning
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1/2 tsp powdered erythritol or allulose, or a few drops of liquid stevia (optional, to round off the tang)

## Steps

1. **Mix the base.** In a jar with a tight lid, combine the balsamic vinegar, red wine vinegar, mustard, garlic, oregano, salt, pepper, and sweetener if using. Shake or stir until the salt dissolves.
2. **Add the oil.** Pour in the olive oil, close the lid, and shake hard for 20 to 30 seconds, until the dressing looks thick and cloudy. The mustard keeps it from separating right away.
3. **Taste and adjust.** Dip a piece of lettuce in it. Add a pinch more salt if it tastes flat, a splash more vinegar for more tang, or a little sweetener if it is too sharp.

## Notes

- The balsamic is the only real source of carbs (about 2.7 g per tablespoon). Brands vary, so check the label, and skip balsamic glaze or reduction, which has several times the sugar.
- For even fewer carbs, use 1 tbsp balsamic and 3 tbsp red wine vinegar.
- Olive oil firms up in the fridge. Let the jar sit out for 10 to 15 minutes, or run it under warm water, then shake again.
- Keep it in the fridge and use it within a week.
- Good on the romaine side salad with the [Bacon and Cheese Smothered Chicken Thighs](blackstone-smothered-chicken-thighs.md) if you want a change from burger sauce.
- Also works as a quick marinade for chicken thighs (30 minutes to a few hours) before they go on the griddle.
