# Kimchi Chicken Fried Rice

Soy-ginger chicken thighs and crispy kimchi fried rice, all on the griddle, topped with a runny egg and furikake. Works with fresh rice, so no need to cook it the day before.

- **Method:** Blackstone griddle, plus a pot or rice cooker for the rice
- **Time:** 30 minutes marinating (or overnight), then about 1 hour
- **Serves:** 6
- **First made:** Not yet (planned November 2026)

## Ingredients

**Chicken and marinade**

- 3 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- 1/4 cup plus 1 1/2 tsp soy sauce
- 1 1/2 tbsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 1/2 tbsp minced ginger
- 1 1/2 tbsp minced garlic
- 1 1/2 tsp five spice powder
- 1 1/2 tsp Tabasco or crushed red pepper
- 1 1/2 tbsp lime juice

**Rice**

- 2 cups dry long-grain or jasmine rice (about 6 cups cooked)
- 1 large red onion, sliced
- 8 to 10 mini sweet peppers, sliced
- 1 1/2 cups chopped kimchi, plus 3 tbsp of its juice
- 3 tbsp garlic butter
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 2 tbsp avocado oil

**To serve**

- 6 eggs (one per person)
- Furikake
- Sesame seeds
- 1/4 cup mayo mixed with Tabasco and a squeeze of lime

## Steps

1. **Marinate.** Mix the marinade and coat the chicken. Refrigerate at least 30 minutes, or overnight.
2. **Cook the rice drier than usual.** Rinse the rice until the water runs mostly clear. Cook it with about 1/4 cup less water than the package says.
3. **Cool the rice fast.** Spread the hot rice thin on a sheet pan and let the steam escape for 10 minutes. Then put the pan in the fridge, or the freezer, for 20 to 30 minutes while you cook the chicken. It should feel cool and dry, not sticky.
4. **Preheat.** Heat the Blackstone with one side on medium-high and the other on medium, about 10 minutes.
5. **Cook the chicken.** Oil the medium-high side. Lay the thighs flat with space between them and cook 5 to 6 minutes per side, until 175°F inside. Rest them 5 minutes, then slice.
6. **Cook the vegetables.** On the medium-high side, cook the onion and peppers for 3 minutes. Add the kimchi and its juice and cook 2 minutes more.
7. **Fry the rice.** Add the rice, garlic butter, soy sauce and sesame oil. Toss everything together, then spread it wide and thin. Leave it alone 3 to 4 minutes to crisp, then flip it in sections and crisp again.
8. **Fry the eggs.** On the medium side, fry one egg per person, keeping the yolks runny.
9. **Serve.** Pile the rice on plates, top with sliced chicken and an egg, then finish with furikake, sesame seeds and the spicy mayo.

## Notes

- Day-old rice still crisps best. If you have it, skip steps 2 and 3.
- Spreading the rice thin on the griddle matters more than the cooling time. Thick piles steam instead of crisping.
- Go by the 175°F reading, not the clock.
- Leftover rice reheats well on the griddle the next day.
