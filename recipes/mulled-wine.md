# Mulled Wine

Warm red wine steeped with orange, cinnamon, clove, and star anise. The fall and winter drink for a cold evening, and it makes the whole house smell like the holidays.

- **Method:** Stovetop, gentle simmer (slow cooker variant included)
- **Time:** 5 minutes prep, 15 to 30 minutes steep
- **Serves:** 6
- **First made:** October 2026

## Ingredients

- 1 bottle (750 ml) dry, fruity, inexpensive red wine such as Merlot, Zinfandel, Garnacha, or Côtes du Rhône
- 1 orange, sliced into rounds, plus extra slices for garnish
- 1/4 cup brandy (optional, adds warmth and depth)
- 2 to 4 tbsp honey, maple syrup, or sugar, to taste
- 3 cinnamon sticks, plus extra for garnish
- 4 whole cloves
- 2 star anise
- 4 green cardamom pods, lightly crushed (optional)
- 3 or 4 thin slices fresh ginger (optional)
- A few apple slices or a small handful of dried cranberries (optional)

## Steps

1. **Combine.** Put the wine, orange slices, brandy, 2 tbsp of the sweetener, and all the spices in a saucepan over medium heat on the induction stovetop.
2. **Heat, but do not boil.** Warm until it is steaming and small bubbles form at the edge of the pan, then stop. Boiling drives off the alcohol and turns the spices bitter. Aim for roughly 160 to 175°F.
3. **Steep.** Turn the heat to low, cover, and let it steep for 15 to 30 minutes.
4. **Taste and adjust.** Add more sweetener if needed. It should taste like warm spiced wine, not dessert.
5. **Serve.** Ladle through a fine sieve into mugs. Garnish each with an orange slice and a cinnamon stick.

## Notes

- Whole spices only. Ground spices make it cloudy and gritty.
- Skip heavily oaked or tannic wines. A cheap, fruity bottle works better than a nice one here.
- If you keep it on the heat longer than about 30 minutes, fish out the orange slices. The pith turns bitter with long steeping.
- Slow cooker: combine everything, cook on low for 1 hour, then switch to warm for up to 3 hours.
- Make ahead: steep, strain, cool, and refrigerate up to 3 days. Reheat gently without boiling.
- Keto version: sweeten with allulose or monk fruit and skip the apple and dried cranberries. A 5 oz pour of dry red runs about 3 to 4 g carbs.
- Alcohol-free: swap the wine for 2 cups unsweetened cranberry juice plus 2 cups apple cider, or use pomegranate juice. Drop the brandy and go lighter on the sweetener.
