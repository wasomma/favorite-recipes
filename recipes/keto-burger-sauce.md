# Keto Burger Sauce

Creamy, tangy, sugar-free burger sauce that comes together in five minutes. About 1 g net carbs per serving.

- **Method:** No cook, mix and chill
- **Time:** 5 minutes, plus 30 minutes to chill
- **Serves:** 8
- **First made:** August 2026

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup mayonnaise (avocado oil or full-fat)
- 2 tbsp sugar-free ketchup
- 1 tsp yellow mustard
- 2 tbsp dill pickles, finely chopped
- 1 tsp pickle juice
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/4 tsp garlic powder
- 1/4 tsp onion powder
- 1/2 tsp powdered erythritol or allulose (optional, for classic sweetness)

## Steps

1. **Mix the base.** In a small bowl, whisk together the mayonnaise, sugar-free ketchup, and mustard until smooth.
2. **Add pickles and seasonings.** Stir in the pickles, pickle juice, smoked paprika, garlic powder, and onion powder. Add the sweetener if you want that classic fry-sauce sweetness. Taste and adjust: more pickle juice for tang, more paprika for smokiness.
3. **Chill.** Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes so the flavors meld.

## Notes

- Check your ketchup label. Regular ketchup runs 4 g or more of sugar per tablespoon, so a sugar-free version (Heinz and Primal Kitchen both make one) is what keeps this keto.
- No sugar-free ketchup: use 1 tbsp tomato paste plus a splash of vinegar and a pinch of sweetener.
- For a smokier, barbecue-leaning version, add a few drops of liquid smoke or a dash of Worcestershire.
- Keeps about a week in the fridge.
- Doubles as the salad dressing for the [Bacon and Cheese Smothered Chicken Thighs](blackstone-smothered-chicken-thighs.md).
